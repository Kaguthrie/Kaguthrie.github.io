Machine Learning Classification of Breast Cancer Subtypes Using Gene Expression Data — ATP Summer Research Grant, 2026

Conducted an undergraduate research project investigating the use of machine learning to classify breast cancer molecular subtypes from gene-expression data. I worked with microarray data obtained from the Gene Expression Omnibus (GEO), including GSE20685, and developed a preprocessing pipeline for mapping probes to genes, handling duplicate measurements, integrating sample metadata, and preparing expression data for analysis. I explored dimensionality reduction and statistical feature-selection techniques such as PCA and ANOVA and investigated machine-learning approaches for distinguishing breast cancer subtypes. The project required integrating concepts from computer science, statistics, machine learning, bioinformatics, and cancer biology.
